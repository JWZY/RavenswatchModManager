Your rework, under the lens.

Review of the supplied 0.4.2 package. Findings concern observable wiring and player-facing promises; they are not a verdict on balance.

What is actually present

Blood Reserve
Basic Attack Speed key verified. Rarity values are 10/15/20/25%; the nested condition checks blood ratio >0.5 and hero appearance. The maximum-blood operation no longer reads the old capacity bonus.
Onslaught
Three-second readiness timer. DASH routing and a dedicated SPECIAL-category damage component using the bite multiplier and 100/125/150/175% selector. ATTACK-based damage multiplication is absent from that operation.
Seductive Provocation
Still a directionless DASH action in Update6, with a six-second cooldown. Dash-end activation was requested earlier but is not established by this package.

The Onslaught percentages are observed package values, not a new recommendation. Earlier conversation left those percentages undecided.

What the checks can and cannot prove

Both entities parse and round-trip byte-for-byte. The full link checker cannot pass with this partial package: it reports unresolved inherited dependencies (453 errors plus one warning for Base; 17 errors for Update6). Those counts are not a count of mod defects. There is no pristine binary comparison, no complete asset tree, and no gameplay or multiplayer test.

Full cooked replacements can shadow changes from other mods or later patches. Retain a source recipe and exact game-version provenance before treating this as a reproducible release.

Questions worth carrying into the next pass

Does the resource pay you back?

Blood powers multiple abilities, but capacity, spending, health fallback, and the new high-blood bonus reward different states. A healthy resource loop should make the choice legible. A tradeoff is useful; a payoff that rarely becomes available is harder to justify.

Does “POWER” mean the same thing everywhere?

Injection bridges abilities already. Its reward depends on whether it inherits the relevant POWER behavior. Damage category, base formula, held-cast bonuses, and on-hit effects must be inventoried separately.

Can Carmilla maintain her own prerequisites?

She has personal bleed sources, so the kit is not inherently teammate-dependent. The concern is whether refreshing them disrupts the channel they are meant to support.

Does a new bridge displace an existing one?

The edited Onslaught could connect SPECIAL, movement, and bite talents. It also shares DASH with Puncture and the earlier Seductive proposal. Their selection order and charge consumption matter as much as the damage percentage.
